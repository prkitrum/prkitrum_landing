FAQ

The questions that come up before the first call

Do you take over projects other agencies started?
Yes. We regularly take over systems other vendors started or abandoned. We start with a technical assessment before committing to a rebuild vs. a refactor.
Can you stabilize an AI-generated codebase?
Yes. AI-Code Handoff & Stabilization is one of our core service lines. We audit it, take ownership of the outcome, and stabilize it before it becomes a production incident.
What company size do you work best with?
Mostly funded, Series B+ product companies, roughly 50 to 300 people, with a live, business-critical system where a bad call is expensive.
How do you price an engagement?
Time & Materials, Fixed, Monthly Run Rate, or Fixed without Benefits: the commercial model adapts to the engagement, not the other way around.
